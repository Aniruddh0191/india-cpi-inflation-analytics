# Findings Summary

## Q1 — May 2023 normalized category share
Pan, Tobacco & Intoxicants is the highest normalized analytical share at 9.34%; Transport & Communication is the lowest at 7.64%. These are normalized shares, not official expenditure weights.

## Q2 — YoY CPI trend
The project’s annual YoY table records 2020 at 7.57%, the highest displayed value. The 2023 value is partial through May.

## Q3 — Food inflation
Food and Beverages CPI moved from 175.53 in June 2022 to 179.67 in May 2023, peaking at 180.10 in October 2022. Spices had the largest positive 12-month category change (+16.34%), while Oils and fats had the largest negative change (−15.04%).

## Q4 — COVID-era changes
From February to March 2020, overall CPI changed by −0.76 percentage points, healthcare by −0.04 pp, food by −1.76 pp, and Essential Services by −0.18 pp. From March to June 2020, overall CPI changed by +0.39 pp, healthcare by +0.67 pp, food by +0.16 pp, and Essential Services by −0.50 pp.

## Q5 — Imported oil and CPI
Milk and products recorded the strongest absolute observed correlation with imported-oil monthly price changes (−0.482). This is an association in the selected study period, not proof of causation.
